8//! Extended Translation Control Register - EL1
9//!
10//! Provides control over the translation table walks for stage 1 of the EL1&0 translation regime.
11//! This register is present only when FEAT_TCR2 is implemented and FEAT_AA64 is implemented.
12//! Otherwise, direct accesses to TCR2_EL1 are UNDEFINED.
13
