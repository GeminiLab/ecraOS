Module domain

Expand description

Scheduling-domain metadata and CPU membership.

A domain owns policy and accounting while physical queues remain owned by CPUs. The bounded bitmap is intentionally local to the task layer for Stage 2B and may later become a lock-free membership structure after a memory-ordering proof.
